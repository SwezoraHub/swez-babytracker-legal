Privacy Policy

Swez BabyTracker
Effective date: October 4, 2026

Swez BabyTracker is designed to help parents and caregivers record and organize information about their baby's daily activities and growth.

We take privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed by Swez BabyTracker, how that information is used, and how users may control their information.

1. Information You May Enter

Depending on the features you use, Swez BabyTracker may allow you to enter information such as:

  • Baby profile information
  • Date of birth or age information
  • Growth and measurement information
  • Weight information
  • Sleep records
  • Feeding records
  • Diaper records
  • Other information that you voluntarily enter into the app

Please do not enter information that you are not authorized to provide.

2. Health and Baby-Related Information

Some information entered into Swez BabyTracker may relate to a baby's health, growth, nutrition, sleep, or general care.

This information is intended for personal tracking and organization. Swez BabyTracker is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

Information shown by the app should not be treated as a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

3. How Data Is Used

Information entered into the app is used to provide the app's tracking and organizational features, such as displaying records, summaries, history, measurements, and other functionality selected by the user.

We do not use baby records for advertising purposes.

4. Our Storage Practices

Swez BabyTracker is designed around a local-first and user-controlled data model.

Swez BabyTracker does not operate its own general-purpose cloud database for storing users' baby records.

Information may be processed temporarily on the user's device while using the app in order to provide the requested functionality.

The exact storage behavior of a particular version of the app may depend on the features enabled in that version.

5. Optional Cloud Backup

Swez BabyTracker may provide an optional feature that allows a user to connect their own Google Drive or another supported cloud-storage service for backup and restoration of their baby records.

When a user chooses to connect a cloud-storage service, the user's authorization is required before the app can access or create the relevant backup data.

Cloud-stored information is stored in the user's own connected cloud-storage account rather than in a Swez BabyTracker-owned database.

Users are responsible for maintaining access to their connected cloud-storage account.

6. Google Services

Certain features may use Google services, including Google Drive and Google Play Billing.

Google services may process information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

When Google Drive connectivity is used, the app only requests the permissions necessary for the intended cloud-storage functionality.

7. Premium Purchases

Swez BabyTracker may offer optional Premium features through Google Play Billing.

Purchasing Premium gives the user access to the specific Premium features described in the app and on Google Play.

A Premium purchase does not constitute a purchase of cloud storage, automatic data retention, automatic backup, or a guarantee that baby records will be recoverable after an app is uninstalled, deleted, or otherwise loses locally available data.

Premium access and baby-data storage are separate services.

8. No Advertising

Swez BabyTracker does not display third-party advertising.

We do not use baby records for targeted advertising.

9. No Separate Swez Account Required

Swez BabyTracker does not require users to create a separate Swez BabyTracker username or password for normal app use.

Certain optional services, such as connecting a cloud-storage provider, may require authorization with that third-party service.

10. App Uninstallation and Data Recovery

Uninstalling an application may remove data that is stored only locally on the device.

Users should not assume that a Premium purchase automatically preserves or restores baby records after an uninstall or reinstallation.

Where supported, users should use the app's available backup or export functionality to maintain a copy of important records.

11. Data Sharing

Swez BabyTracker does not sell users' baby records.

We do not share baby records with advertisers.

Information may be processed by third-party services when such services are required to provide a feature that the user intentionally chooses to use, such as Google Play Billing or connected cloud storage.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the app's architecture and services to protect information processed by Swez BabyTracker.

However, no electronic storage, transmission, or third-party service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Children's Privacy

Swez BabyTracker is intended for parents, caregivers, and other adults managing baby-care records. The app is not intended to encourage children to create or manage their own accounts.

14. Your Choices

Users may choose what information they enter into the app and whether to use optional cloud-storage or backup features.

Users should also review the privacy and security settings of any third-party service they choose to connect to the app.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, or data practices change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
